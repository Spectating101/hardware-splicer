## Core doctrine

Hardware-Splicer can create economic value in two modes:

1. **HS-as-product** — sell or license the bounded engineering system itself.
2. **HS-as-factory** — keep HS internal and use validated capability reuse to create smaller products whose marginal engineering cost can be measured against a blank-slate path.

The modes are complements. Neither may claim evidence owned by the other.

The commercial question is not "Which story sounds larger?" It is:

> Which conversion path has enough measured evidence to justify the next irreversible spend while preserving the value of the underlying capability asset?

## Shared evidence boundary

Both modes inherit the same truth rules:

- model proposals are not physical truth;
- deterministic verification is not physical proof;
- physical evidence is revision- and artifact-bound;
- human authorization is explicit and does not silently carry across revisions;
- unresolved identity remains unresolved;
- private capability reuse must not become unsupported public truth;
- failed experiments remain evidence;
- estimates must never be recorded as measurements.

The frozen unseen SPI corpus, evaluator, and answer-bearing experiment remain untouched until the first genuine provider-backed run is sealed.

## Mode A — HS as a product

### Product hypothesis

A bounded engineering environment that helps an operator move from requirements and evidence to reviewable hardware candidates while preserving provenance, uncertainty, deterministic checks, physical-evidence gates, and human authority.

Candidate users are hypotheses: embedded/prototyping teams, validation and test-fixture teams, hardware labs, NPI/support teams, and engineering organizations needing inspectable AI assistance rather than unconstrained autonomous design.

Potential value must be measured, not asserted: less repeated evidence discovery, fewer unsupported substitutions, lower pre-fabrication review effort, less repeated work across related variants, less avoidable physical retest, and easier reconstruction of engineering decisions.

### Product boundary

The product is not "AI certifies hardware" or "AI replaces the engineer." The bounded product is evidence-aware reasoning plus canonical project/revision state, deterministic checks, selective evidence invalidation, revision-bound physical evidence, explicit authorization boundaries, and an auditable operator workflow.

### Evidence gates before stronger claims

1. frozen software/harness campaign green;
2. genuine unseen provider execution sealed;
3. physical execution closed for at least one case;
4. independent operator evidence;
5. repeatable packaging/deployment boundary;
6. external pilot evidence before claiming external usefulness;
7. repeated external use before claiming broad production readiness.

Until those gates close, HS remains an internally validated research/product asset rather than a generally proven commercial product.

## Mode B — HS as an internal derivative-product factory

### Factory hypothesis

A validated capability should reduce the marginal engineering burden of an adjacent product when identity, provenance, interfaces, selective evidence invalidation, deterministic verification, and physical authority are carried forward correctly.

The output sold to a customer may be a much smaller product. HS itself can remain invisible.

### Factory loop

1. start from a physically validated capability manifest;
2. identify an adjacent product candidate;
3. apply opportunity gates before spending;
4. freeze requirements and acceptance criteria;
5. freeze baseline capability/evidence/artifact inventory;
6. freeze reuse prediction before result leakage;
7. build the reuse path;
8. run the preregistered blank-slate comparator when required;
9. close physical evidence and authority;
10. measure development economics;
11. separately measure product COGS, price, and distribution assumptions;
12. package, hold, or kill the derivative.

### What counts as arbitrage

The advantage is not merely "the AI wrote something faster." It is a repeatable gap between engineering an adjacent product with validated reusable capability and a controlled blank-slate path.

A credible claim needs measured evidence: retained engineering obligations/artifacts, retained evidence, invalidation quality, human active hours, model/tool and service cash cost, physical retest burden, authority violations, and development variable-cost comparison.

Production unit economics are separate. A cheap development path can still produce an uneconomic product.

## Choosing between modes

Prefer **HS-as-product experimentation** when the platform workflow itself repeatedly creates operator value without bespoke engineering dominating delivery.

Prefer **HS-as-factory experimentation** when validated capability has strong adjacency to a smaller product, the product has a clear acceptance test, and reuse advantage can be measured while HS stays internal.

Prefer **both** only when the same experiment genuinely adds evidence to both. Never create duplicate work just to maintain two narratives.

## Irreversible-spend rule

Before meaningful inventory, fabrication batch, incorporation, paid acquisition, or external commitment, record what evidence changed, which mode benefits, what uncertainty the spend resolves, why a cheaper experiment is insufficient, and the kill condition for a negative result.

The default while evidence is incomplete is to preserve option value.
