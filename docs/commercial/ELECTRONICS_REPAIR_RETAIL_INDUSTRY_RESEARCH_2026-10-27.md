# Electronics & electrical-engineering repair business — Taiwan market deep dive (2026-10-27)

**Research scope.** Real-world service and asset-conversion business opportunities for Hardware Splicer (HS), first in Taiwan (especially Taoyuan, Hsinchu, Taiwan industrial and SME customers), with selective China procurement, Indonesia expansion and international repair-policy reference. This is a **desk-research and hypothesis document**. Published provider prices and policies are observations, not a statistically representative price survey. No supplier or customer interviews, quoted conversion funnel, ownership rights, case outcome or earned income has been verified.

## 1. Executive investment thesis

**Do not treat 'electronics repair' as a single retail category.** The relevant sectors are:

1. Consumer phone/tablet repair: visible ticket prices, rapid part replacement, high retail competition, sensitive batteries and customer privacy.
2. Consumer and small-office computers: diagnostics, SSD/RAM/PSU upgrades, operating-system recovery, PCBs, data and refurbished machine sales.
3. Small-business operational electronics: POS terminals, barcode scanners, label/receipt printers, Wi-Fi/network gateways, power supplies and HMI terminals. Value is the customer workflow and service continuity.
4. Industrial electronics: PLCs, inverters, servo drives, motor controllers, I/O modules, HMIs, semiconductor boards, obsolete components and power electronics. Higher diagnostic complexity, proof and liability threshold.
5. Refurbishment and inventory arbitrage: buy defective/used units, recover/repair, validate condition and sell, rent or operate. Risk comes from adverse selection and inventory, not simply parts.
6. Preventive servicing, field maintenance and contractual uptime: recurring customer relationships but high availability and liability expectations.
7. Commercial service intelligence and sourcing: triage, evidence collection, parts compatibility, standardized quotation/risk gates, quote comparison, condition records, technical brokerage and measured economic decisions.

**Decision:** HS should initially target a **business-facing diagnostic / technical sourcing / decision-support workflow with a verified independent repair partner**, not launch a commodity walk-in phone shop or claim autonomous industrial repairs. Medium-term opportunities are (i) industrial/SME obsolete parts matching and replacement routing, (ii) 24V-safe test/reference-gated low-voltage refurbishments, and (iii) accountable sourcing and repair-cost intelligence sold as a service. The latter must prove customer willingness to pay; mere software capability is not a business.

**Potential differentiation still unproven:**
(a) fast and accurate identity/fault verification using images, layout, test logs and reverse engineering;
(b) multi-route decision that compares repair **against replacement, salvage, own use and resale**;
(c) source-bound cost and component compatibility reconciliation;
(d) rigorously replayable evidence/acceptance reports that support customer warranty and audit;
(e) learning loop from actual fault, parts, time and outcome records across unrelated equipment types.

Prove superiority on *held-out actual repair cases* rather than an in-house synthetic benchmark.

## 5. Unit economics — illustrative models only, NOT Taiwan averages

### A. Consumer PC successful service example

*Assumed* fee NT$2,200; parts NT$650; technician labor 2h×NT$400 = NT$800; admin/payment/logistics NT$180; warranty allowance NT$200.

- Contribution if finished, billed and paid: **NT$370**, before storefront fixed cost and customer acquisition.
- If 85% of inbound jobs succeed and the other 15% yield **no billable fee** but incur NT$480 diagnosis/logistics each: expected contribution **NT$242.50/intake**.
- 20 such inbound jobs/month produce just NT$4,850 before rent, marketing, licensing, bench amortization and cash taxes. A high-volume retail business is needed or economics are weak.

### B. Industrial module successful service example

*Assumed, not observed*: billed successful repair NT$9,000; parts NT$1,400; diagnosis/repair plus QA 6.5h×NT$500 = NT$3,250; shipping NT$300; warranty allowance NT$750; other overhead NT$400.

- Contribution per **successful and paid** module: **NT$2,900**.
- On failed module: 3h diagnosis×NT$500 + return freight NT$300 = **NT$1,800 loss** when charging no-fix/no-fee.
- At 70% repair success: **NT$1,490 expected contribution per incoming module**.
- At 30% success: **minus NT$390 per intake**.
- Break-even conditional success probability: roughly **38.3%**, under this specific assumed accounting. Real labor, warranty and high-risk component costs may differ drastically.

**Critical:** A quoted NT$9,000 repair is not proven typical market pricing. DAFENG's public floor and equipment-value formula explain the market mechanism, *not* our assumed ticket sizes.

### C. Inventory-led refurbishing example

*Assumed*: acquire NT$2,500; parts NT$800; labor 4h×NT$450 = NT$1,800; QA NT$300; freight NT$200; warranty allowance NT$500; total unconditional cost = NT$6,100; sale if working NT$7,600; 70% successful yield; failure recovery NT$500.

- Risk-adjusted receipts: 0.7×7,600 + 0.3×500 = **NT$5,470**.
- Expected contribution **negative NT$630/donor**. To break even at the same yield and unconditional costs requires **NT$8,500 completed sale price**.
- Real conditional failure costs could be lower (or higher), but this example proves that apparently cheap hardware can be an uneconomic business.

### 5.1 Universal acceptance formula

`E[contribution/intake] = P(repair)*[collected_fee - parts - labor - QA - freight - warranty - payment costs] + (1-P(repair))*[diagnostic_revenue - failure_assessment_cost - return_transport] - allocated acquisition/marketing/admin overhead`.

For refurbished resale, include **purchase price, condition-dependent yield, failed-donor recovery, storage, returns, capital costs, and actual completed sale prices**. For assets owned and operated, use measured productivity and discounted cash flow, not fictitious resale prices. Avoid double counting the same salvaged component as sale revenue and input into another build.

### 5.2 Floor for labor, not a billing rate

Taiwan's statutory 2026 minimum compensation is **NT$29,500/month or NT$196/hour**. Private-sector pay data suggest electronic equipment repair employees frequently earn materially above minimum; for instance, Taoyuan's 2026 electronic-repair vacancy advertised NT$35–45k/month. The model's NT$400–500/hour is an **economic burden/opportunity-cost assumption**, not a statutory rate or verified contract rate.

## 6. Physical capability and quality gates

### Skills and bench
- Identification: OEM model/variant verification; board markings, BOM, connector mapping, damage evidence and datasheets.
- Non-powered: microscopy, continuity, known-good comparison, trace inspection, corrosion and shorts.
- Safe low-voltage functional bench: calibrated DMM, current-limited isolated DC source, appropriate protection, oscilloscope/logic analyzer as needed, load and temperature measurement.
- Component repair: ESD-controlled mat/grounding and handling, solder/hot air, inspection, controlled rework for qualified personnel.
- Release: functional test against original application, burn-in appropriate to device, power consumption, firmware licensing, data sanitization, failure/rework and warranty register.
- **No mains, high-voltage capacitors, battery packs, RF/high-power, safety-critical drives or medical equipment without appropriately qualified physical personnel and the necessary facilities.** Low-voltage operating claims must still be independently measured.

Industry control references: [IPC-7711/7721 rework/repair](https://www.ipc.org/ipc-7711-ipc-7721-endorsement-program) and [ANSI/ESD S20.20](https://www.esda.org/store/standards/product/314/ansiesd-s20-20-2021/). These are useful reference frameworks; HS has no claimed certification or qualified repair authorization.

### Equipment spending strategy
Do not buy a full repair shop on day one. First secure legitimate low-risk test articles and a qualified human partner. Source equipment around the actual experiment. Taiwan catalog example: a combined hot-air/solder station is advertised around NT$1,300, whereas premium equipment can exceed NT$8–16k. Price alone does not establish calibration, safety, operator expertise or suitability. Source: https://www.dado3c.com/categories/%E7%B6%AD%E4%BF%AE%E5%B7%A5%E5%85%B7%E6%9D%90%E6%96%99

## 7. Operations: real end-to-end repair business

1. Intake: customer identity/account, device serial, photos, privacy/data permissions, power/battery risk, symptom and contract.
2. Eligibility triage: lawful ownership, safety, repairability and original manufacturer warranty implications.
3. Paid or clear free inspection: diagnosis contract, time cap, consent, deposit if any.
4. Evidence: known-good comparison, live measurements only if qualified, error codes, functional tests.
5. Route: repair, component-level rework, send to a qualified subcontractor, exchange unit, refurbish, retire/recycle, reject.
6. Source and quotation: exact SKU, reputable vendors, freight/duty, MOQ, lead time, alternatives with compatibility check.
7. Transparent quote: itemized service/parts, warranty, expected turnaround, salvage/failure disposition.
8. Written authorization: no automatic repair or material purchases.
9. Execution and QA: authorized technician and calibrated/suitable test procedure; logs, no model-generated safety release.
10. Hand-over: serial matching, test evidence, receipt, data handling, acceptance signature.
11. Aftercare: issue/rework tracking, return reserves and clear warranty scope.
12. Economics feedback: actual collected cash, failed diagnostic hours, quote acceptance rate and cash/material inventory reconciliation.

**Operational KPIs:** qualified leads, first-response/quote time, quote acceptance, paid diagnosis uptake, physical success by fault family, warranty return rate, first-time fix rate, cost and collected contribution per *incoming* device, tech-hours/device, parts fill rate, days in inventory, working-capital days, percent accepted with independent full-device QA. Do not use 'jobs accepted', AI accuracy or modeled margin as substitutes for customer outcomes.

## 8. Legal / regulatory and personal feasibility — Taiwan

- **Classification:** official statistics include computer/peripheral repairs (9521), communication (9522) and electronic/home electronics repairs (9523). Business registration may use **JA02010 電器及電子產品修理業** plus relevant resale/installation categories where applicable. Industry classification itself is not a grant of specific permits. https://www.stat.gov.tw/StandardIndustrialClassificationContent.aspx?Level=4&PID=OTUyMQ%3D%3D&RID=8&n=3144&sms=11195 and https://findbiz.nat.gov.tw/fts/company/94218305
- **Consumer documentation:** Taiwan consumer authorities tell customers to ask for diagnosis fees, warranty/parts terms, repair receipts, serial identity, approval of extra costs, and data/privacy protection. Build this into HS before serving customers. https://www.iachu.nat.gov.tw/zh-tw/open/ArticleNewsPage?a=22767&c=1&edate=&p=2&q=&sdate=
- **Product safety:** BSMI requires assessment of regulated goods before sale; a commercial modifier/assembler or importer may assume product-inspection obligations. Applies on a product-specific basis, not a blanket rule that every repaired circuit is a new product. https://www.bsmi.gov.tw/wSite/lp?ctNode=8318 and https://www.bsmi.gov.tw/bsmiGIP/wSite/ct?ctNode=9925&mp=1&xItem=119997
- **Waste vs genuine reusable goods:** regulated electronic/information waste and physical handling can require specialized registration/permits. This is not covered by a routine repair business classification. https://recycle.moenv.gov.tw/Services/QA/3 and https://oaout.moenv.gov.tw/Law/LawContent.aspx?id=FL015604
- **Student/foreign work status:** overseas Chinese students require work permissions and ordinarily face **20h/week during term** (excluding winter/summer break). A student permit must not be assumed to authorize self-employment or operation of a sole-proprietor repair shop. As of February 2026, qualified Taiwan graduates with approved extended residence have a special post-graduation work-permit exemption, but company ownership/registration and regulated activities may have other requirements. Confirm individual eligibility with competent authorities before paid operations. https://english.moe.gov.tw/cp-96-25407-74f3-1.html and https://ezworktaiwan.wda.gov.tw/en/cp.aspx?n=8CE17C2739F9AB35&s=939E48F42B607BE5
- **Apple IRP:** genuine Apple part/independent-program eligibility requires formal business/certified technical infrastructure; the US official application page is not evidence of Taiwan enrollment eligibility. https://support.apple.com/irp-program
- **Regulatory trends:** EU repair directive started member-state application in **July 2026**, covering only regulated product categories. It is a long-term international demand/access signal, **not Taiwanese law**. https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/consumer-protection-law/directive-repair-goods_en

## 10. Four pilot hypotheses (falsifiable, with stopping rules)

**Pilot R1: Local retail computer triage partner**
- Five anonymized previously resolved cases from a willing independent shop, including failed/no-fix jobs.
- HS predicts root-cause category, inspection hours, parts family and repair-vs-replace recommendation *before seeing outcomes*.
- Compare against shop baseline on unseen cases; require actual technician review. Success = lower net diagnostic time or better quote precision with no unsafe calls. A shop must express willingness to pay, refer clients, or repeatedly use results. Otherwise kill monetization.

**Pilot R2: Barcode/POS/label equipment**
- 5–10 exact-model, lower voltage devices (including working and faulty test devices), no speculative bulk purchase.
- Evaluate line/USB/I/O failure diagnostics, USB cable/power variants, replacement prices and test documents.
- Success = at least one genuine repair/exchange/sourcing outcome plus independent user value or settled B2B invoice. Kill if new commodity hardware dominates fully landed repair.

**Pilot R3: Obsolete industrial component matching**
- Partner submits 10 anonymized unfulfilled parts requests (OEM part numbers and hardware revisions; no proprietary or unsafe control code).
- HS finds exact/verified compatible parts with dated quotation and clear uncertainty, compares outsourced repair, replacement and new production alternatives.
- Success = ≥3 correctly sourced viable alternates **validated by qualified human** and one paid purchase/referral or explicit procurement intent. Kill if exact matching and customs delivery are not competitive.

**Pilot R4: Own-operated industrial controller**
- For existing or legitimately testable ARK-1123/Wyse-class device, define one productive workload and power/uptime/maintenance cost.
- Test under safe low voltage with correct PSU and no required direct factory interface.
- Success = measured weekly work output/cost avoidance exceeding full burdened cost. Kill if Optiplex or commodity module is cheaper or offers greater reliability.

**Do not** pilot hazardous/mains/industrial actuator repair autonomously. Send those to qualified service partners.

## 11. 30/60/90-day evidence gates

**Day 0–30 (market discovery):** 15 structured customer-discovery calls/interviews across repair shops, industrial maintenance and independent SME integrators; 10 anonymized historical case files; 10 technician/replacement quotes; separate failed and successful historical repairs. Find one partner willing to share anonymized resolved tickets and test outcomes. Verify student status/business legality before any compensated work.

**Day 31–60 (measured technical performance):** replay 10 held-out actual cases with blinded predictions, partner evaluation and measured technician time/cost; one affordable low-voltage physical demonstration only if qualified test infrastructure is available. Target >10% reduction in burdened quote/diagnostic time while preserving zero high-severity unsafe output and >95% correctly attributed source references; those are **experimental thresholds, not promised performance**.

**Day 61–90 (commercial proof):** secure 1–3 **legally permissible** paid or written-intent partner tests, measure attributable revenue and burdened cost (including no-fix cases), obtain real source-based receipts and repeat interest. Advance only if real invoice or measured useful operating output closes the economic loop; else retain HS as internal arbitrage/research tool, not a repair business.

## 12. Risks and missing research

1. No reliable Taiwan repair industry-wide TAM/SAM/SOM estimate was produced from these sources. **Do not invent market size** from aggregated quote platforms.
2. This is source-based desk research with company self-reports; no mystery shopping, customer calls, sold-price dataset or field QA.
3. Warranty and liability vary by product, fault type and contractual customer relationship.
4. Chinese component sourcing may reduce input cost but adds authenticity/lead-time/tax and liability risk. The current China market dataset is sparse and based largely on aggregators; not bankable quotes.
5. Indonesian repair pricing exists (e.g. published 2026 laptop service ranges) but lacks comparable, source-verified commercial contracts and parts/wholesale supply cases. https://panduanplus.com/biaya/biaya-servis-laptop/
6. A high-value industrial repair often requires genuine physical expertise, reference boards and proprietary test fixtures; HS cannot substitute a generic LLM or photo for that.
7. Financial payoff is fundamentally **per-intake expected cash contribution** under success/failure probabilities, not gross invoice minus one component.

## Bottom line

The compelling market opportunity is **not 'open a generic electrical repair retail shop'**. It is to use HS for technical-economic triage, supply-chain sourcing, condition verification and reliable use/repair/replacement decisions—initially alongside already capable repair businesses. That has lower initial physical and liability exposure, creates a clear information advantage experiment, and can later grow into verified refurbishing, productive assets, vendor exchange inventory, or licensed hardware manufacturing.

**Status:** market and competitor hypotheses established from direct published sources; **no customer willingness to pay, physical repairs, successful sales or unit profits proven**.
