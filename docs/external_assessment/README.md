## One-sentence identity

**Hardware-Splicer is an evidence-constrained AI engineering agent that helps prepare real hardware for fabrication while refusing to turn guesses into physical authority.**

## The evaluation story

Hardware-Splicer should not be evaluated as “an AI that generates hardware.” Its differentiator is the separation between reasoning and authority:

> **AI proposes → deterministic systems constrain → bench evidence decides → human authorizes.**

The embedded AI can reason, propose, ask, block and remain unresolved. It cannot silently promote model confidence into component identity, verified electrical truth, physical evidence or fabrication/power/motion/release authority.
