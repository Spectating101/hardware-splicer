## Proposed future title

**Evidence-Constrained Multimodal Agents for Reproducible Physical Engineering Experiments**

Alternative:

**Measuring Evidence and Authority Boundaries in AI-Operated Physical Engineering Workflows**

## Scientific question

> What evidence and authorization controls are required for multimodal AI agents to conduct useful physical engineering workflows without promoting unsupported inference into physical action?

## 200-word proposal summary

AI agents are increasingly capable of reasoning over technical evidence and operating tools, but physical engineering creates a scientific control problem: model inference can be useful without being sufficiently grounded to justify fabrication, power-on or other consequential action. Hardware-Splicer is an existing model-independent experimental environment that separates semantic agent reasoning from deterministic constraints, provenance-bearing evidence, revision state and scoped human authorization. I propose a controlled study combining adversarial agent evaluation with revision-bound physical validation. Agents will encounter cases with partial evidence, identity conflict, tool failure, analogy traps and stale revisions. We will measure unsupported consequential-action attempts, correct abstention, false blocking, task completion, human intervention and recovery behavior. A physical extension will bind candidate artifacts and measurements to exact revisions so that simulated, stale or unrelated evidence cannot silently satisfy physical claims. Claude credits would support repeated multimodal/agent runs, sensitivity analysis and analysis of failure behavior. The intended outcome is a reproducible benchmark and experimental framework for determining when AI-generated engineering reasoning has sufficient evidence to cross into physical authority.

## Proposed study structure

### Aim 1 — Agent behavior under adversarial evidence

Compare reference and constrained agent conditions across the frozen corpus using the canonical matched-condition protocol.

### Aim 2 — Physical promotion boundary

Test when model-derived proposals are permitted to transition into revision-bound physical actions/measurements. The reference condition remains advisory/dry-run for consequential action; physical execution is limited to an explicitly bounded human-authorized protocol.

### Aim 3 — Recovery and human oversight

Measure how agents recover after evidence, tool or physical failures and when human intervention remains necessary.

### Aim 4 — Reproducible evaluation artifact

Release a benchmark, failure taxonomy and trace methodology where licensing/provider/institutional rules allow.
