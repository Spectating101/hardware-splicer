# Portal Handoff

When a submission portal is opened, use the prepared venue packet and Blowback manifest rather than drafting inside the portal.

## Human-controlled fields/actions

Keep these human-controlled:

- login / 2FA / CAPTCHA;
- legal identity or student-status attestation;
- originality/authorship attestation;
- adviser confirmation;
- payment/travel authorization;
- terms acceptance;
- final submit.

## Prepared upstream

Everything else should already exist in this package:

- project title;
- short/medium/long summary;
- innovation and technical contribution text;
- limitation disclosure;
- deck/paper source;
- demo narrative;
- evidence state;
- repository/package links.

Do not improvise stronger claims to satisfy a required field. If a required field cannot be answered truthfully from canonical data, stop at the human gate and record the blocker.
