# Do Not Rewrite the Project Per Venue

Hardware-Splicer has one project identity and one evidence state.

A venue may change:

- title/subtitle;
- ordering;
- word/page count;
- rubric emphasis;
- requested next step.

A venue may not change:

- what is proven;
- what is pending;
- what is not claimed;
- whether live/physical evidence exists;
- the authority model;
- frozen experimental cases after observation.

This file exists to prevent submission pressure from turning package adaptation into architectural or evidentiary drift.
