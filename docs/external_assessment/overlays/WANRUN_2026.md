## Recommended identity

**Hardware-Splicer: Auditable AI Agent for Semiconductor Validation Hardware**

## Competition story

WanRun should receive the most product/innovation-oriented version of Hardware-Splicer.

### Problem

AI-generated hardware output can appear finished before the exact component, compatibility evidence, revision state or physical behavior is known. This creates expensive risk in validation and lab-support hardware.

### Solution

Hardware-Splicer gives a general-purpose AI agent a bounded engineering environment. The AI performs semantic reasoning, while deterministic constraints, evidence provenance, exact revision state and scoped human authority decide what is allowed to progress toward fabrication or power-on.

### Innovation

Do not pitch “LLM + PCB design” as the novelty. Pitch:

- model-independent MCP operation;
- deterministic engineering/evidence shell around the model;
- unresolved-state handling;
- exact revision/artifact binding;
- fail-closed real-vs-simulated evidence;
- explicit human physical authority;
- adversarial trace/replay evaluation.

### Application potential

Lead with semiconductor test and validation support hardware:

- adapter boards;
- carrier boards;
- fixtures;
- lab/NPI support hardware;
- repeated engineering tasks where mistakes are costly and evidence reuse has potential value.

## Suggested application summary

Hardware-Splicer is an auditable AI-agent environment for semiconductor validation support hardware. General-purpose AI can accelerate engineering reasoning, but model confidence alone cannot establish component identity, electrical compatibility or physical correctness. Hardware-Splicer separates AI proposals from deterministic constraints, provenance-bearing evidence, exact revision state and human physical authority. Its current platform exposes 193 canonical engineering operations through MCP and includes a frozen ten-case adversarial SPI-flash evaluation corpus. The objective is not an infallible autonomous engineer; it is a practical engineering workflow in which uncertainty remains unable to silently become fabrication or power-on authority.

## Patent / defensibility framing

If asked about intellectual-property potential, emphasize system architecture and workflow mechanisms rather than making unverified patentability claims:

- separation of semantic agent reasoning from authority-bearing deterministic state;
- revision/evidence invalidation logic;
- constrained physical-authority transitions;
- model-independent engineering gateway + replay/evidence architecture.

Use the wording **“potentially protectable system/workflow mechanisms subject to prior-art review”**, not “patentable” as a fact.

## Demonstration

Use the canonical three-minute sequence:

1. imperfect engineering evidence;
2. agent enters through MCP/API;
3. deterministic/evidence constraints preserve unknowns or block unsafe progression;
4. candidate/package becomes auditable;
5. physical authority remains closed without real evidence.

## Evidence boundary

Show PROVEN/PENDING/NOT CLAIMED visibly. A judge should not discover the limitations only during Q&A.
