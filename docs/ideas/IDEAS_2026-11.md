## Parked: keep the notes, no work now

- **Foldable SSH terminal / dual-screen pocket cyberdeck** (5-9 Nov). Two screens on a straight hinge, thin enough for a pocket; later an AYN Thor-class handheld with the controls replaced by a split QWERTY keyboard. The discussion concluded that a finished uConsole, or a used Fold 4 at about NT$10k, beats a DIY build on price; thickness mattered more than weight. Decision recorded: this is not a cyberdeck company. Keep it as an HS case study.
- **Agent driving any connected 3D printer** from a desktop running HS, printer-agnostic (23 Oct). Waits on a printer.
- **DIY kits** (for example a small generator kit for students or professionals), and a modular low-voltage electronics test kit as "one complete product using the whole portfolio" (14-20 Nov). Waits on the first physical build.
- **Submarine drones**, alongside the air drone (19-20 Nov). Physical-only; nothing in the repo.
- **Functional synthesis**: start from the function ("a water filter") and derive the device, instead of starting from a known template (23 Oct). Partly covered by the digital engine's requirements.

## The business layer: consolidating elsewhere

Discussed 19-26 Oct and again in November: e-waste salvage as an arbitrage source, a Taiwan hardware-arbitrage report, electronics repair retail in Taiwan and Indonesia, a commercial-intelligence pipeline with a supplier-quotation gate (a dated written quote, exact variant, quantity, condition, shipping, tax, testing, repair and warranty before any landed cost counts).

- That pipeline was PR #214. It is **closed, not merged**. "Arbitrage" still appears in 118 files on `main`.
- A repair-business trial found 8 of 12 cases with local listings, 14 supplier directories, 87 broad matches, and **0 verified profitable opportunities**. The missing pieces were live marketplace prices and exact SKU-level supplier matching.
- Recommendation: business-finding moves to one place outside HS, with HS supplying the engineering checks. A lead counts only with a real buy price, a real sell price and a named buyer.
