A simulation of this design

The retained HS-3G-B geometry, mass distribution and maker thrust table parameterize a six-degree-of-freedom PyBullet model. A simulation controller performs takeoff, square tracking, yaw, gust recovery and landing. Playback shows recorded states; it does not move the aircraft along a scripted flight path.

Representation, with stated assumptions

Components and blades are simplified representations. Motor lag, drag and controller gains are assumptions; sensors provide ideal state feedback. This is a rigid-body study, without flexible-frame, CFD, radio, battery-chemistry or INAV firmware execution. Current is a table-based estimate. No physical performance is claimed or required to complete this digital deliverable.
