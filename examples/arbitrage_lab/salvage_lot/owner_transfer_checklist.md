# Legitimate electronics salvage / purchase handover checklist

**Private working document only; never put completed private fields or donor identifying information in public GitHub.** This blank template conveys no ownership, commercial authorization or legal advice.

## What to ask the current owner / recycler

- Are you the authorized owner or officer entitled to transfer these items (not municipal collection property, school fixed assets awaiting official disposal, employer property, or a former tenant's belongings)?
- May I receive or **purchase** the listed equipment for *inspection, repair, dismantling, reuse and potential resale of working equipment or components*? Please state any restrictions clearly. A no-resale gift MUST NOT be used for commercial recovery.
- Which specific devices are included? Get model and number of units, age, photos, and "power-on observed" vs "untested." Do not imply working hardware from labels.
- Is any battery swollen, leaking, overheated or punctured? Is any mains-exposed equipment, CRT, microwave, refrigerant, or damaged EV pack present? **Do not accept dangerous or regulated materials for casual salvage.**
- Are there storage media or user data? Owner should retain or securely erase storage where possible. Avoid inspecting files or transferring personal data; have a consented erasure/chain-of-custody plan.
- Are there pickup/transport, recycling, export, audit or asset-release requirements? Onward recycling of rejected electronics must use an appropriate lawful channel.
- Is the lot actually available, and what are the transfer terms? Confirm only before traveling, not from stale forum posts.

## Minimal private record fields

Date/time, current owner/authorized agent, transfer authority, quantity/model/photo summary, declared missing parts and damage, purchase price, transport, any resale restriction, approval or invoice/reference, storage data-erasure/handling instructions, unsafe exclusions and destination. Have both sides acknowledge the terms. For organization-owned assets, require its official documented disposition process.
