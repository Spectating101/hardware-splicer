# Digital design revision pilot

A hypothetical workshop uses an original Ender-3 as a pen plotter. Start with the HS reference study using a 10 mm pen, a 100 × 80 mm drawing viewport and a 3 mm pen lift. Adapt it for a 12 mm cylindrical marker and a 125 × 80 mm viewport. Keep the same carriage and lift. This is an internal rehearsal using a pre-engineered family.

## Digital acceptance

- The revised STEP adapter and clamp must change. Check the modeled diameter against the requested marker and retain the original carriage interface.
- Verify the emitted drawing path stays in the revised viewport and on the bed, with the requested lift. Keep the original and revised requirements for comparison.
- Reopen and verify the export, including the revised model and drawing preview. Record unresolved physical questions for the recipient.
- Report machine time by phase. Record human engineering effort, independent review, model cost and prior family setup as unknown where unmeasured. Only a real buyer can accept a commercial order.
