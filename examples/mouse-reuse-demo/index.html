Could an old mouse become
a foot button?

That’s the idea: start with something ordinary, work out how to reuse it, and make something you can try.

Computer-only demonstration. There is no physical mouse or foot pedal being tested here.
1 · What we start with

An old wired mouse

Public product information is our starting point. Its actual condition and insides are unknown.

2 · What we want

“Can I press something with my foot to trigger one action on my computer?”

Reuse the mouse. Don’t start by buying another controller board.

3 · What the agent worked out

Keep the mouse intact first.

These are summaries of the saved agent study—not a new AI conversation running on this page.

Preferred proposal

Let a pedal press its button

Keep the mouse’s electronics and cable. A separate frame would carry your foot’s weight, rather than the mouse itself.

Alternative, more unknowns

Move the button to a pedal

Keep the electronics but extend a switch. First, someone would need to identify the real contacts and check the circuit.
