# Build or buy the HS soldering cell

19 November 2026. Decision for a studio starting with a six-pin through-hole connector and an empty bench. Prices are single-unit observations, except where a quantity is explicitly multiplied. The [editable cost inputs](cost-inputs.csv) retain seller currency and separate listed prices, allowances and labor. Taiwan and Indonesia have separate comparisons. US variant prices supply an engineering benchmark. They require freight, tax and regional power substitutions before becoming a landed budget. The workcell remains digital.

## Starting with reclaimed hardware in Taiwan

For a six-pin J1 job, the first useful asset is a board holder that keeps the connector seated and exposes the solder side. A scrap-card board outline, two edge stops and a clip can rehearse loading and access cold. An illustrative NT$300 (US$9.11) ceiling covers small fixture supplies if suitable offcuts or fasteners are unavailable. Shared fabrication access can defer a printer purchase. Fablab Taipei lists public use under its rules with user-supplied materials. [Fablab Taipei](https://www.fablabtaipei.org/)

The studio owns zero donors. In an isolated copy of the [original Ender-3 pen study](../ender3-penplotter-digital/README.md), six 0.8 mm pen strokes replaced the stock drawing through the existing prompt-drawing input. Their centers came from the hashed board record in [header.json](header.json). The local replay returned six separate marks on 2.54 mm pitch and 12.70 mm total span. All stroke midpoints matched the mapped board centers and each XY transfer ran at the modeled 3 mm lift. The result applies to the original Ender-3 geometry on paper. It leaves donor wear, fixture reseating, physical registration, joint quality and operator time unmeasured.

An indexed Taiwan Ender-3 ask was NT$1,500 (US$45.53). A separate untested V2 at NT$2,000 was delisted with zero stock. The V2 would require its own carriage check. Assuming NT$200 for pickup and NT$500 for adapters or wear parts, the NT$1,500 donor costs NT$2,200 (US$66.77) before incremental labor. Against the existing US$159 motion-kit budget (NT$5,239), the difference pays for about five hours of extra work at NT$600/hour. Two extra hours leave NT$1,839 savings. Six extra hours consume the advantage. Both paths still need their common tooling. The reclaimed motion assembly earns its place only if the fixture trial shows a positioning need and the donor meets the same job requirements. **Against a manual bench:** at $24/h, removing 30, 60 or 120 seconds of operator attention per accepted board saves $0.20, $0.40 or $0.80. Recovering the full $1,334.91 then takes 6,675, 3,338 or 1,669 boards respectively. If both routes require the same new iron/power/cable and extractor, the incremental investment drops to $1,030.95 and the one-minute case takes 2,578 boards. Time loading, inspection, cleaning, retries and rework on both routes. A robot running for a minute only saves that minute if the operator can do something else.

**Adding the arm:** using the US$299 sold-out listing as a price benchmark and six incremental integration hours gives another $443, or $1,777.91 for the base cell. At 15 seconds of net operator time saved per board, the arm alone requires 4,430 transfers to cover that increment. An inspection or sorting job can share this investment. Pallet sensing or new guarding above the base allowances would increase it.

**Selling the station.** The expanded model gives a pretax break-even price of **$1,348** and a **$1,874** price for a 25% project margin at ten paid sales. The earlier $1,532 estimate yields **10.7%** after modeled manufacturing losses, inbound and outbound freight, customer acquisition, warranty, and shared development. The arm-equipped cell needs **$2,541** for the same 25% target. These are US-dollar planning scenarios before company overhead and tax. Regional delivery, realized sale prices and production performance remain unverified. ### The specialization supported by our own design

HS's [revision-bound J1 pad record](header.json) has six identical X coordinates and five Y steps of 2.54 mm. The end-to-end span is **12.70 mm**. A fixed lateral tool setting, one indexing direction and a retracting head could cover this job. First constrain the inexpensive XYZ donor to that motion in software. Design out an axis only if a cheaper mechanism can meet the same acceptance test and the change pays for its engineering.

Centering the alignment datum on the row halves its maximum lever arm from 12.70 to 6.35 mm. A hypothetical 1° rotation would shift the farthest pin laterally by about 0.111 mm, versus 0.222 mm when located from pin one. Translation, real fixture play, tip width, barrel lean and the intended lead-side offset add to that error. The present cold trace targets hole centers. A candidate revision should compute the actual lead-side tip point, retain the connector during inversion, check feed-nozzle clearance, and reject an off-datum pallet. A physical coupon would eventually have to establish wetting, bridges, first-pass yield and touch-up time. This narrower job has a measurable advantage to pursue and a cheap manual reference to beat.
