# Engineering library by product family

Use the family name to filter the 620-row [concept index](concept-index.csv). Each row identifies its assumed components, goal and configuration ID. The 197 base IDs group battery, Nano and Pico suffixes while retaining named platform differences. The references below identify a commercial alternative or component for one representative job in each family. They do not price every device or validate its assumed parts.
