# What should HS design first?

**18 November 2026 · 27 modeled offers.** The most promising reusable family in this sample is a scanner-specific film adapter. Buyers already purchase individual formats, the product is small enough to ship, and several variants can share scanner geometry. The existing inspection dock is the cheapest digital entry. A 6×6 camera-scanning carrier leads the physical margin scenarios, with weaker evidence of buyer volume. **Selection.** The lower-entry design family is the weighing platform for Taiwanese tea and Indonesian seaweed. Compare two real tray sizes, model off-center loading and track hours for the second accepted variant. The closest water-robotics fit is the Taiwanese reservoir sampler. Start with an editable hull, instrument bay and bottle loading simulation. The Indonesian depth-survey version is a second instrument package with its own calibration and positioning requirements. A price for a survey-grade finished boat is a poor guide to the price of the sample boat or either module. None of these niches yet has both verified yearly sales and a landed, supported HS bill of materials from which to rank profit.

For a **digital-only company experiment**, I would publish the existing inspection package first and direct the first new design study toward **one V600 Kodak Disc adapter**. Record design and review time to establish the first scanner interface. Then change the film format while keeping the scanner fixed and measure the incremental time. At 25 sales per variant and a $35 delivered price, even one extra engineering hour at $24 would exhaust that variant’s 30% margin cushion. That is a concrete test of whether HS makes low-volume product families economical.

## Model assumptions and reading the CSV

- Physical goods use the earlier screen’s 8% payment/channel and 5% returns allowances. Recurring work is costed at US$24/hour or NT$360/hour. The CSV separately identifies materials and purchased modules, machine time and rejects, labor, and domestic packaging and freight. Cost allowances for new designs are engineering hypotheses. No batch quotes, measured yields, or paid HS orders are available. [Earlier model and local prices](market-screen-2026-11-18.md)
- Digital sales assume a direct Gumroad credit-card checkout, 10% + $0.50 platform fee and 2.9% + $0.30 processing, plus a modeled 5% refund allowance and 3–5 minutes of average support per paid download. Marketplace discovery, advertising and currency conversion can produce different results. Support time and price matter more than file storage. An extra 15 minutes of support consumes $6 per sale. [Gumroad fee details](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees)
- Contribution equals delivered price after percentage deductions, less flat fee, materials, machine time, recurring labor and fulfillment. First-design costs, test equipment, overhead, tax adjustments and acquisition still have to be paid. “Affordable hours” uses the positive balance after retaining 30% of revenue on 25 sales. It is a spending limit, with zero shown when the product already misses target. Twenty-five sales is a scenario, not a forecast. Countries are modeled separately.
- The six flatbed adapters deliberately share a $35 delivered test price and $19 direct-cost assumption so the **observed format demand** can be compared without inventing precision. The Disc comparator’s displayed delivered total was $37.70 at the sampled US destination. Production prices and individual format costs could differ. A 6×6 carrier for a camera rig and a 120 holder for a flatbed have different buyers, optics, interfaces and prices.
- The US powered turntable’s $34 materials budget allows approximately $14 for a motor, $6 for a driver, $4 for a controller and $10 for remaining hardware, housing and power connections. It requires a compatible drive and supply selection. The controller listing showed out of stock. Shipping and packaging for both light boxes need actual carton quotes. These are early feasibility models.
