# What should HS design first?

**18 November 2026 · 27 modeled offers.** The most promising reusable family in this sample is a scanner-specific film adapter. Buyers already purchase individual formats, the product is small enough to ship, and several variants can share scanner geometry. The existing inspection dock is the cheapest digital entry. A 6×6 camera-scanning carrier leads the physical margin scenarios, with weaker evidence of buyer volume. ## What entry costs when HS owns no parts

| Entry route | Incremental cash and engineering implication |
|---|---|
| Existing PCB dock digital release | **$0 incremental inventory and listing fee** under the direct-download scenario, using existing tools. One dock and three board carriers are already packaged. At a $9 price, 25 sales fund **2.80 additional engineering hours** at $24/hour while keeping 30% of revenue. Include founder review, fixes and release prep in those hours. Historical development spend is unmeasured. [Existing package](../pcb-inspection-nest/README.md) · [Platform terms](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees) |
| Existing plotter digital release | Same $0 incremental inventory. At $15 and 25 sales, **5.22 additional hours** are affordable on the same basis. A digital-only release can demonstrate the path simulation. Buyers would still have to validate their donor hardware. [Plotter package](../ender3-penplotter-digital/README.md) |
| New scanner-adapter family | Model allows **$3 of material per adapter**. One sold, working V600 with power cord displayed **$329.99 plus $90.43 shipping** to the quoted destination. At $11.45 contribution, **37 units** would recover that $420.42 host example alone, before design, test media or profit. At 25 sales a single variant leaves only **$23.75** above a 30% target. Sharing a genuinely compatible bed reference across variants matters. Digital fit studies can proceed with documented dimensions, with optical verification still open. [V600 sale](https://www.ebay.com/itm/168797630550) · [Disc holder and delivered-price reference](https://www.ebay.com/itm/283794454015) |
| New 6×6 carrier | The model allows **$5 of material and $3 of machine time/rejects per unit**. At 25 sales, about **$240.75** is available for engineering, validation or acquisition while retaining 30%. A camera, macro lens, stand, light and test film are additional if the team needs a physical acceptance setup. Their total is unquoted. [Comparable optical interface](https://valoi.co/en-us/products/easy120-film-holder-for-120-6-6) |
| New indexed turntable in Taiwan | iCShop lists a **28BYJ-48 motor and ULN2003 driver at NT$40** and a **Pico with headers at NT$195**. The model adds NT$200 for bearing, platter, enclosure, power and connections, giving **NT$435 in materials**, before fabrication and testing. At NT$1,290 delivered, contribution is NT$277.30. At a nearby generic competitor’s NT$809 it becomes **−NT$141.17**. The catalog parts still need torque, backlash, repeatability and power checks. [Motor and driver](https://www.icshop.com.tw/categories/motor-drive) · [Controller](https://www.icshop.com.tw/categories/pi-pico) · [Taiwan turntables](https://24h.pchome.com.tw/search/電動旋轉展示台) |

For a **digital-only company experiment**, I would publish the existing inspection package first and direct the first new design study toward **one V600 Kodak Disc adapter**. Record design and review time to establish the first scanner interface. Then change the film format while keeping the scanner fixed and measure the incremental time. At 25 sales per variant and a $35 delivered price, even one extra engineering hour at $24 would exhaust that variant’s 30% margin cushion. That is a concrete test of whether HS makes low-volume product families economical.

## Model assumptions and reading the CSV

- Physical goods use the earlier screen’s 8% payment/channel and 5% returns allowances. Recurring work is costed at US$24/hour or NT$360/hour. The CSV separately identifies materials and purchased modules, machine time and rejects, labor, and domestic packaging and freight. Cost allowances for new designs are engineering hypotheses. No batch quotes, measured yields, or paid HS orders are available. [Earlier model and local prices](market-screen-2026-11-18.md)
- Digital sales assume a direct Gumroad credit-card checkout, 10% + $0.50 platform fee and 2.9% + $0.30 processing, plus a modeled 5% refund allowance and 3–5 minutes of average support per paid download. Marketplace discovery, advertising and currency conversion can produce different results. Support time and price matter more than file storage. An extra 15 minutes of support consumes $6 per sale. [Gumroad fee details](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees)
- Contribution equals delivered price after percentage deductions, less flat fee, materials, machine time, recurring labor and fulfillment. First-design costs, test equipment, overhead, tax adjustments and acquisition still have to be paid. “Affordable hours” uses the positive balance after retaining 30% of revenue on 25 sales. It is a spending limit, with zero shown when the product already misses target. Twenty-five sales is a scenario, not a forecast. Countries are modeled separately.
- The six flatbed adapters deliberately share a $35 delivered test price and $19 direct-cost assumption so the **observed format demand** can be compared without inventing precision. The Disc comparator’s displayed delivered total was $37.70 at the sampled US destination. Production prices and individual format costs could differ. A 6×6 carrier for a camera rig and a 120 holder for a flatbed have different buyers, optics, interfaces and prices.
- The US powered turntable’s $34 materials budget allows approximately $14 for a motor, $6 for a driver, $4 for a controller and $10 for remaining hardware, housing and power connections. It requires a compatible drive and supply selection. The controller listing showed out of stock. Shipping and packaging for both light boxes need actual carton quotes. These are early feasibility models.
