# P026 Fixed-angle split-keyboard supports

Wingless Moonlander-envelope keyboard support pair at 13.73°. Assumed typing positions retain 32.5 mm landing margin.

[Candidate SVG](trial.svg) · [Rejected SVG](rejected.svg) · [Editable dimensions and assumptions](../designs.json) · [Replay measurements](../results.json)

## Setup

Use the manufacturer wingless envelope of 170 × 170 mm per half. The 155 mm base span, 41.55 mm rise and 18 mm plug allowance are trial dimensions. Fit two wedge carriers under each board and set positive stops against measured safe underside lands. The section uses mirrored cross-sections and assumes a clean contact coefficient of 0.45. [ZSA dimensional FAQ](https://www.zsa.io/moonlander/faq/what-are-the-physical-dimensions-of-the-moonlander) · [ZSA 1:1 flat printout](https://www.zsa.io/1-1.pdf)

## Negative control

A 67 mm base places the outermost modeled typing load 11.5 mm beyond the support edge. The 155 mm base provides 32.5 mm margin.

## Physical acceptance

Transfer underside contact zones and plug clearances from both actual keyboard halves, then test typing and slipping across the target supports.

The catalog models a 25-sale result of NT$ 724.76 at its proposed price. This is a planning estimate. The digital result applies to the geometry or synthetic operating assumptions in this trial.
