# P004 Printed PCB dock with one OpenLog carrier

SparkFun DEV-13712 carrier with 0.35 mm pocket allowance, 12 mm card service travel and 7.5 mm projected camera margin.

[Candidate SVG](trial.svg) · [Rejected SVG](rejected.svg) · [Editable dimensions and assumptions](../designs.json) · [Replay measurements](../results.json)

## Setup

Outline dimensions are 15.24 × 19.05 mm from Eagle layer 20. Header JP2 runs across the lower edge when the board is drawn y-down. Socket U3 and its card extension lie on the upper edge in that projection. Open the service relief to the carrier edge, raise the board on edge pads and fit a removable spring corner. The pocket allowance, card pull requirement and field are engineering assumptions. Measure the full populated height and underside component placement before making the final pads. [SparkFun Eagle board file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sparkfun/OpenLog/master/hardware/OpenLog.brd) · [SparkFun product page](https://www.sparkfun.com/sparkfun-openlog.html)

## Negative control

Reducing the service travel to 4 mm loses 8 mm of pull clearance. The provisional requirement is 10 mm.

## Physical acceptance

Fit a populated DEV-13712 and re-seat it ten times. Confirm the card clears the holder and photos preserve framing.

The catalog models a 25-sale result of NT$ -3,203.00 at its proposed price. This is a planning estimate. The digital result applies to the geometry or synthetic operating assumptions in this trial.
