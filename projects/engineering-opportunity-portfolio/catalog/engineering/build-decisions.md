# What the engineering changes about the build decision

All money below uses the catalog's Taiwan workshop assumptions of NT$600/hour and NT$32.947 per US dollar. The 25-sale result includes modeled parts, build labor, delivery, fees, one-time design and prototype costs for that offer. The internal-use comparison includes one design effort, a prototype allowance and one production unit. None of the products has measured demand or physical acceptance in this edition.

| Decision | What the trial found | Economics and next gate |
|---|---|---|
| **Build the switch tester as the first repeatable shop tool.** [P028](trials.html#P028) | Revised Omron holders clear by 0.195 mm in the tolerance model. The contact evaluator classified 448 synthetic cycles. A small holder has only 0.020 mm extra retaining land above the design minimum. | NT$800 parts, NT$1,550 repeat build, NT$7,150 first internal unit. At NT$3,990 and 25 sales, modeled profit is NT$35,938 (US$1,091), or 56.3% of total outlay. The US$300 commercial listing has three stations. Measure force, stroke, retaining land and actual assembly minutes before quoting. |
| **Build the green coffee divider if the shop actually needs a compatible divider.** [P151](trials.html#P151) | The revised feed passes the 5% split-error criterion across ten synthetic 1 kg bead samples at 4.20% worst error. Adding an 8% feed bias drove the error to 11.40%. Actual flow through a full-width feed scoop determines whether the machine is useful. | First build NT$8,890 (US$270) against the selected NT$15,156 (US$460) starting ask. Potential first-use saving NT$6,266 (US$190). Selling 25 at the proposed local price loses NT$2,975. Measure the actual bean size, calibrated sample mass and feed bias. |
| **Try the rain recorder before buying that class of logger.** [P130](trials.html#P130) | A 20 ms contact lockout captured 78 modeled tips at both tested rain rates. A 10 s lockout undercounted the faster stream by 50.47%. The trial does not measure the bucket. | First build NT$11,235 (US$341) against a US$495 selected ask. Modeled first-use saving NT$5,074 (US$154). Selling 25 at NT$2,250 loses NT$8,150. Calibrate the real bucket at two delivery rates and cost the complete outdoor installation. |
| **Develop the indoor camera rover around a clearance requirement.** [P030](trials.html#P030) | The proposed loaded shell clears a 75 mm mock roof and 6 mm threshold by 6.8 mm. Raising the shell from 61 to 70 mm causes 2.2 mm of interference. The rover's camera, traction and runtime remain open. | NT$9,250 parts, NT$10,650 repeat build, NT$55,900 first internal unit. Selling 25 at NT$16,900 models NT$34,650 profit (US$1,052), 8.9% ROI. The NT$10,400 listing is a chassis. Source and time a complete comparable camera system before claiming savings. |
| **Buy the standard follower arm for the first cell. Develop the tooling it holds.** [Arm P049](trials.html#P049) · [Fingers and nest P047](trials.html#P047) | Moving the cold transfer station brought it within kinematic reach. A 1.5° shoulder zero error still produced 4.79 mm of tool error. The custom gripper/nest study independently shows a centered support layout within its 1 mm modeled handoff allowance. | NT$7,088 (US$215) for the selected calibrated arm against NT$7,235 (US$220) for one repeat build and NT$20,090 (US$610) including launch. The arm loses NT$48,610 at 25 sales. The tooling models NT$16,788 profit at 25. Price the parallel mounting mechanism before applying the tooling return to this arm. |
| **Keep the underwater camera vehicle at tank-study stage.** [Vehicle P031](trials.html#P031) · [Guard set P032](trials.html#P032) | The revised vehicle's worst modeled upward reserve is only 42.66 g equivalent. Tether loading, added mounts or absorbed water could consume it. The guard study found 0.05 mm nominal host-fit margin at its limiting tolerance condition. | The full vehicle has NT$8,300 parts, NT$11,000 repeat cost and NT$67,300 (US$2,043) first cost. Its 25-sale result is **−NT$83,380** (−US$2,531). The six guards earn only NT$563 at 25 sales. At NT$600/hour, **2.25 extra minutes per guard set** consume that batch profit. Check a named host and tank loads before funding either product. |
| **Treat price pressure and small fit allowances as separate reasons to pause.** [8 mm film P016](trials.html#P016) · [Punching cradle P232](trials.html#P232) | The revised 8 mm window retains only 0.059 mm of image margin. The punching guide clears its modeled exit slit by 0.75 mm. Both can pass their digital geometry checks. | At the catalog target price the 25-sale results are +NT$4,425 and +NT$11,563. Capping each price at its selected comparable ask changes those to **−NT$950** and **−NT$3,381**. Measure the film and compare delivered prices and product scope before interpreting either as a sales opportunity. |
