# P034 Pick-and-place staging carrier

Transfer staging carrier with three fiducials, 5 mm local nozzle clearance and 6 mm travel clearance above clamps.

[Candidate SVG](trial.svg) · [Rejected SVG](rejected.svg) · [Editable dimensions](../designs.json) · [Replay results](../results.json)

## Setup

The plate uses a 140 × 95 mm synthetic stage coupon with four transfer bores. Adapt the four-hole pattern to actual measuring datums, or fit adapter feet on the existing stage accessory. Use an assumed 90 × 50 mm board, with three registration fiducials and a 12 mm projected nozzle envelope. In-plane registration, approach height and clearance near the toe clamps are checked using the saved drawing. [Opulo LumenPnP board validation workflow](https://docs.opulo.io/openpnp/v4-2/ftp-validation/board-validation/)

## Negative control

Moving the right toe 10 mm toward the placements changes the closest clearance from 5 mm to 3 mm of interference.

## Physical acceptance

Measure the four real mounting positions and board height with a gauge. Dry-run all placement targets with actual nozzle geometry and verify there is no contact at the assumed safe-travel plane or during the final approach.
