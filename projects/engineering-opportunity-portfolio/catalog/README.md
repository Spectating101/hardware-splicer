## What is worth building

For the first internal machine, use the switch tester to answer a real bench question and measure how much time the next switch holder takes to design. For a first manufactured product, the tablet keyguard has a small parts bill and a plausible return if several customers can use exactly the same layout. For a field product, the water sampler has both a concrete sampling job and an Indonesian buying alternative. The standardized SO-101 arm is a purchase candidate. Making the fingers and locating nest for the parts it must handle has better modeled economics.

## Calculating a return

All base runs use 25 units produced and sold, identical design, NT$600 per active labor hour, and zero unbudgeted selling or fixed costs. Every row has its own price, fee, parts, process, labor, delivery, engineering and prototype allowance. Earlier US offers retain US domestic fulfillment. New offers use a Taiwan workshop and domestic delivery. The Taiwan dollar is the accounting currency. Conversions use the November 18 closing/reference rates of NT$32.947 and Rp18,252 per US dollar. Local-market comparisons need an actual shipping, duty and tax quote to establish a landed saving. [Taiwan central bank](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/en/lp-700-2-1-40.html) · [Bank Indonesia](https://www.bi.go.id/en/statistik/informasi-kurs/transaksi-bi/jisdor/default.aspx)

| Measure | Formula and use |
|---|---|
| Production cost for one repeat | Parts + process/reject allowance + active assembly minutes × hourly rate / 60. Group allowances for new products are itemized in the cost records. |
| Launch and first internal unit | Launch = design hours × hourly rate + prototype allowance. First internal unit = launch + one repeat build. This budgets a separate prototype and a finished unit. No selling fees or outbound customer delivery enter the internal-use comparison. |
| Contribution and margin | Contribution = sale price − production cost − delivery − fixed selling fee − percentage fees/reserve. Margin = contribution / selling price. |
| Payback and ROI | Break-even sales = launch / positive contribution, rounded up. Batch profit = revenue − all modeled batch costs. Return on cost = batch profit / those costs. Income tax, financing, unallocated rent, unbudgeted acquisition, special certification and new machinery are outside the base. |
| Uncertain sell-through | Stocked downside charges production on all 25 physical units, discounts 17 sold units by 20% and assigns remaining units zero recovery in the period. Made-to-order charges production only for those 17. Downloads incur per-order support on fulfilled orders. |
| Comparable purchase and price-match | Same job means the same intended operation. Fit, quality and performance still require testing. Selection prefers a same-job offer, then Taiwan, then Indonesia, then the lowest convertible ask within that tier. All other observed alternatives remain accessible. EUR figures are preserved in native currency. Prices compared are advertised and exclude unquoted landed costs. |
| Engineering limit | The returns CSV gives the maximum design hours that 25 sales will pay for after the prototype budget. A negative value means sales at the target price cannot even fund that prototype. The additional allowable hours per order exhaust contribution before recovery of fixed costs. |

## Files

- [Product master](products.csv) identifies the buyer, delivered scope, parts and manufacturing allowances, evidence, references and acceptance test for every offer.
- [Calculated returns](returns.csv) covers one, ten, 25 and 100 sales, downside stocking, made-to-order, first and repeat internal costs, achievable-price sensitivity and engineering headroom.
- [All priced listings](all-purchase-listings.csv), [selected comparisons](purchase-comparisons.csv) and [regional evidence](regional-listings.csv) retain native pricing and scope caveats. A missing price leaves the purchase-saving field blank.
- [Build versus buy at each seller's price](buy-vs-build-by-listing.csv) applies the same workshop cost to all 246 observations. It shows how changing the supplier or market changes first-build savings, the quantity needed to justify design, and the result at a matched selling price. A different-scope listing retains its advertised price and a blank savings estimate.
- [Sourcing and design reuse](sourcing-and-reuse.md) connects named parts, donor economics and public design references to candidate products.
- The ZIP includes the earlier workbook, [620-configuration index](../archive/concept-index.csv) and [engineering evidence](../archive/engineering-records.md) in the sibling `archive/` directory. Open `catalog/index.html` after extraction. Seller pages need a network connection. Run `python3 rebuild.py` in the catalog directory to regenerate the CSV returns and offline viewer from the editable CSV inputs. The workbook is a separately editable edition snapshot.
- To build the delivery ZIP from the Hardware Splicer checkout, run `python3 projects/engineering-opportunity-portfolio/package_catalog.py` from the repository root. Use `--output /path/to/catalog.zip` to write another copy. The packager takes the current catalog, previous edition and supporting records from their source directories. Identical input bytes produce an identical ZIP with the same Python and compression runtime.

## Verification and host usage

An independent calculation reconciled all 270 financial models, 219 itemized parts totals and the original 51 scenarios. The workbook's 4,590 return-sheet formulas were checked in the saved file. An offline browser run checked the base, downside, made-to-order and price-capped calculations against the CSV values, as well as search, product detail, country filters, ranking and narrow-screen layout. This verification covers the archive calculations. Physical builds, vendor quotations and commercial sales remain open.

During research and export, 137 host samples covered 22 minutes 41 seconds at roughly ten-second intervals. Available memory ranged from 11.8 to 17.6 GiB. Swap usage rose from 19.7 GiB at the first sample to the host's 24.6 GiB capacity. The one-minute load peaked at 28.4 on six CPUs. Minimum observed free disk space was 61.67 GiB. Workbook generation and browser verification ran sequentially in separate memory-limited scopes with swap disabled for each. These readings cover all concurrent processes on the host.
