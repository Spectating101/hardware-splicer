# Which machinery product should HS try to supply?

**Research date: 18 November 2026.** Taiwan is the first sourcing and selling scenario. US domestic examples are identified separately. This screen covers four maker and small-shop equipment families where HS has relevant CAD, fixture or machine-design experience. It uses public campaign records, manufacturer statements, product listings and a small number of completed marketplace listings. It cannot establish a market-wide margin or unit-volume leader.

## How much room is there for engineering?

At a proposed **30% contribution target** and 13% combined fees/reserve, only 57% of the sale price remains for all other variable costs. For a full desktop CNC matching a US$349 comparator that is **US$198.93** for the entire landed BOM, assembly, testing, packaging and fulfillment. For the $1,995 pick-and-place comparator it is **US$1,137.15**. These are purchasing ceilings. Full HS machine BOMs, yields and installed-base forecasts are unknown.

- At NT$1,990, the DM-500X kit can spend **NT$826.05** on the optical module while retaining 30%, after its other NT$308.25. Retail is NT$1,260. At NT$1,290 the corresponding Sily ceiling is **NT$427.05** against a promotional NT$521. At NT$990 with that same Sily component, contribution falls to **NT$32.05**. A better vendor price helps only if mechanics, image quality and support burden remain acceptable.
- At NT$499, the standalone nest has **NT$6.18 per sale** left after reserving 30% of revenue. Across 25 sales, that is only **NT$154.50** for NRE or customer acquisition while preserving that target. If it can sell for only the generic holder's NT$280, the modeled contribution is **−NT$34.65** before NRE.
- At US$44 the film carrier has **US$5.08 per sale** above the same 30% target, or **US$127 across 25 sales**. An extra 15 minutes of labor at the modeled rate costs $6 and uses the entire cushion. With the $84.99 bundle price, the maximum light cost is **$25.44** to preserve 30% after $23 of other costs. These thresholds make actual review, rejects, returns and acquisition measurable go/no-go inputs.

## Decision for HS

Start with a digital release of the existing dock and named board carriers as the lowest incremental NRE experiment. Test a film carrier as a second original family if the geometry and physical validation can fit its cost ceiling. A CNC or pick-and-place fixture is a separate compatibility project with a stronger host-machine demand signal but unknown attach rate.

For each candidate, track exact SKU/revision, seller, ship-to region, timestamp, quantity tier and landed offer cost. Recheck promotional prices and obtain a second qualified source. On the sell side track completed transactions by model, condition, bundle and region over a fixed 90-day window, deduplicate relistings and retain concealed Best Offers as unknown. Seller Hub Product Research can supply actual transaction prices where permitted. A self-published catalog can measure model-specific reservations, paid orders and returns; record free downloads separately. Re-rank by **monthly paid units × contribution per accepted unit − acquisition, family engineering and support costs**, with recurring costs counted once. No orders, listing publication, sourcing agreement or physical validation occurred in this study.
