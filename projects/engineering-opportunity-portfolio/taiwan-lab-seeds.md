# Starting an electronics lab with reusable equipment in Taiwan

Research snapshot, 19 November 2026. The studio has no confirmed physical equipment. The proposals below assume access to an existing computer. Taiwan-dollar prices are seller asks. US-dollar equivalents use NT$32.947 per US$1. Pickup, freight, tax where quoted extra, shop space, and hands-on labor can change the economics. [Central Bank closing rate](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/en/lp-700-2-1-40.html)

## First asset and the cost of automation

The first electrical asset I would design is a manual harness board with interchangeable connector ends. A cut-length stop and pinout card help assemble cables. A removable adapter lets the same board check disconnected cables. Candidate first publication: a digital adapter drawing, wiring map, BOM and fault examples for a named connector pair. The exact series, pitch and orientation must be established before making compatibility claims. A standard RJ cable tester already costs roughly NT$520 / US$15.78. Custom connector support is the practical reason to develop another fixture. [Pro’sKit retail reference](https://shop.prokits.com.tw/instruments/1703030013/1703140029/)

| First bench expense | NT$ | US$ | Basis |

| UNI-T UT33D+ meter, common to all projects | 520 | 15.78 | 100Y displayed local store stock. Shipping under its NT$1,200 threshold adds NT$120 for its own order. [Listing](https://www.100y.com.tw/product/126609) |
| Assorted connector kit | 120 | 3.64 | Supplier listing. Check the actual mating part and pitch before choosing. [iCShop](https://www.icshop.com.tw/categories/wire-to-board) |
| Adapter plates, leads, resistors, USB data cable and small hardware | 150 | 4.55 | Illustrative allowance assuming usable offcuts. Increase if starting with no hand tools or fabrication access. |
| Optional Pico H for automatic scanning | 190 | 5.77 | Listed before tax. [PiePie](https://piepie.com.tw/product/raspberry-pi-pico-h/) |
| Manual bench / bench with Pico | **790 / 980** | **23.98 / 29.74** | Excludes transport and any extra tax. Assumes access to tools and a host. If operating multiple projects at once, budget one controller per deployed device. |

At an assumed NT$600 per active engineering hour, DIY automation taking two hours consumes another NT$1,200. If the manual fixture and meter already exist, the incremental investment is NT$1,390 (US$42.19) for the controller and work. One minute genuinely saved per cable is NT$10, giving a break-even of 139 cables. At 30 seconds it takes 278. At five cables per month the one-minute case takes nearly 28 months. These are thresholds to compare with real volume and handling time.

A digital trial used WireViz 0.4.1 on two illustrative 200 mm four-conductor cables. One is straight through and one intentionally exchanges pins 2 and 3. The same check evaluated the 24 possible one-to-one assignments, four single opens and six single pairwise bridges per reference. In each family, an ideal end-to-end continuity matrix distinguished the intended assignment from 33 alternatives. The trial assumes independent conductors, perfect contact and full matrix observation. Physical contact resistance, flex-dependent faults and fixture electronics remain untested. The bundle includes editable source, diagrams, BOMs and the independent model check. [Harness example bundle](four-wire-seed-examples.zip)

## Where the reclaimed stock comes from

Taipei’s public surplus portal exists for government bodies to sell retired property that retains reuse value. A historical National Formosa University disposal inventory includes oscilloscopes, a battery tester, and power supplies. A historical National Synchrotron Radiation Research Center lot included racks, fans, linear stages and electronic modules. These records establish what turns up. Original asset values are historical accounting figures. Neither inventory supplies a current sale price or a functioning-instrument guarantee. [Surplus portal](https://shwoo.gov.taipei/shwoo/newhome/newhome00/faq) · [University inventory](https://gaw.nfu.edu.tw/wp-content/uploads/sites/16/2025/12/%E6%8B%8D%E8%B3%A3%E6%B8%85%E5%86%8A.pdf) · [Research-center lot](https://www.nsrrc.org.tw/chinese/pdf/105%E5%A0%B1%E5%BB%A2%E8%B2%A1%E7%89%A9%E6%8B%9B%E6%A8%99%E5%85%AC%E5%91%8A%28%E9%80%9A%E4%BF%A1%E6%8A%95%E6%A8%99%29.pdf)

Treat each intake as a lot. Record the donor model, usable quantity, test performed, pickup cost, founder minutes and new replacement ask. Store tested parts separately from unverified candidates. The maximum sensible bid is the expected avoided purchase cost of parts the studio will actually use, less pickup, testing, repairs and disposal. At NT$600/hour, 15 minutes of recovery costs NT$150. A new NT$118 weighing sensor pair already costs less than that time. A rack, matched slide assembly or characterized instrument can justify much more attention.
